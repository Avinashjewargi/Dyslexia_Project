Once upon a time there was a little rabbit named Rinu. Rinu lived in a green forest. He was small and soft. He loved carrots very much. Every morning he woke up early. He hopped out of his tiny house. He brushed his ears with his paws. He smiled at the sun. The sun smiled back at him. Rinu liked to play alone. He was shy with other animals. He watched birds flying. He watched butterflies dance. He felt happy but lonely. One day Rinu heard a cry. The sound came from behind a tree. Rinu felt scared but curious. He slowly hopped forward. He saw a baby squirrel. The squirrel was stuck. Its leg was caught in a bush. The squirrel was crying softly. Rinu felt sad for it. He wanted to help. He pulled the bush gently. The bush did not move. He pulled again slowly. The squirrel stopped crying. Rinu used his teeth carefully. The bush broke a little. The squirrel was free. The squirrel smiled happily. It thanked Rinu. Rinu felt proud. His heart felt warm. The squirrel called its friends. Soon many animals came. They clapped for Rinu. The birds sang songs. The deer nodded kindly. Rinu felt shy again. But he also felt brave. From that day Rinu was not alone. He played with friends daily. He shared carrots happily. He learned to help others. He learned to be brave. The forest became brighter. Rinu became happier. And every night he slept with a smile.
