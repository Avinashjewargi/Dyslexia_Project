Competition Structure

Round 1: Abstract Submission

+ Deadline: 20th August 2025
+ Participants must submit two/three pager (excluding intro) an abstract containing
at least the following
o Team members details
o List of team members (team needs to contain at least 3 members)
o Institute details
o List of labs and facilities at your institute that will help you realize the
prototype
o While having a faculty mentor is not mandatory at this stage, it is
encouraged. If you have identified faculty mentor(s), please mention their
brief bio.
o Rough list of components (electronic and mechanical) for your bot
° syeren level block diagram
Rough specifications of your bot
» Battery sizing
» Number and specs of motors
« Approx. Weight of the system
« Details of the controller
« Details of the sensors
« Chassis material
+ Number and specs of wheels
- Dust storage capacity
* Cleaning mechanism
» Storage container sealing
Removal and reattach mechanism for the storage container
